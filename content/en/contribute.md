## Code of Conduct

Our community aspires to treat everyone equally and to value all contributions. We have a [Code of Conduct](/code-of-conduct) to foster an open and welcoming environment.

<section class="contribute-section" id="ways-to-contribute" aria-labelledby="ways-to-contribute-title">
  <h2 id="ways-to-contribute-title">Ways to Contribute</h2>
  <p class="contribute-section__lead">Your choices aren't limited to programming — there are many areas where we need <strong>your</strong> help.</p>
  <div class="contribute-ways-grid">
    <article class="contribute-way-card contribute-way-card--featured">
      <h3>Contribute to development</h3>
      <p>You do not need to understand the entire codebase before contributing — many contributors begin with documentation improvements, bug reports, or small fixes.</p>
      <p>Check out the contribute section of the project you are interested in:</p>
      <ul class="contribute-project-links">
        <li><a href="/openms-lib/#for-developers">OpenMS-lib</a></li>
        <li><a href="/pyopenms/#for-developers">pyOpenMS</a></li>
        <li><a href="/pyopenms-viz/#for-developers">pyOpenMS Viz</a></li>
        <li><a href="/webapps/#for-developers">OpenMS WebApps</a></li>
      </ul>
    </article>
    <article class="contribute-way-card">
      <h3>Developing Educational Materials</h3>
      <p>OpenMS's <a href="https://github.com/OpenMS/OpenMS/wiki">User Guide</a> is undergoing rehabilitation. We're in need of new tutorials, how-to's, and deep-dive explanations, and the site needs restructuring. Opportunities aren't limited to writers — we'd also welcome worked examples, notebooks, and videos. <a href="https://github.com/OpenMS/OpenMS-docs/issues">OpenMS-Docs</a> lays out our ideas, and you may have others.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="contribute-way-card">
      <h3>Website Development</h3>
      <p>Help shape the OpenMS website — fix bugs, improve layout, add content, or polish the experience for visitors.</p>
      <p>The source lives on <a href="https://github.com/OpenMS/OpenMS-website" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">GitHub</a>. Browse <a href="https://github.com/OpenMS/OpenMS-website/issues" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">open issues</a> for ideas, or propose your own.</p>
      <p>For content-only updates without front-end experience, see the <a href="https://github.com/OpenMS/OpenMS-website/blob/main/docs/README.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">website maintainer guide</a>.</p>
      <a class="contribute-way-card__btn openms-lib-btn openms-lib-btn--ghost openms-lib-btn--github" href="https://github.com/OpenMS/OpenMS-website/edit/main/content/en/contribute.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">
        <i class="fab fa-github" aria-hidden="true"></i>
        Edit this page on GitHub
      </a>
    </article>
    <article class="contribute-way-card">
      <h3>Graphic Design</h3>
      <p>We can barely begin to list the contributions a graphic designer can make here. Our docs are parched for illustration; our growing website craves images — opportunities abound.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="contribute-way-card">
      <h3>Community Coordination and Outreach</h3>
      <p>Through community contact we share our work more widely and learn where we're falling short. We're eager to get more people involved in efforts like our <a href="https://discord.com/invite/v9tv5BxPch" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Discord server</a>, <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/company/openms-org" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">LinkedIn</a>, <a href="https://bsky.app/profile/openms.bsky.social" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">BlueSky</a>, and <a href="https://twitter.com/OpenMSTeam" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">X</a> account, organizing events, a newsletter, and perhaps a blog. To get involved, reach out to <a href="mailto:communitymanager@openms.de">communitymanager@openms.de</a>.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="contribute-way-card">
      <h3>Fundraising</h3>
      <p>OpenMS was all-volunteer for many years, but as its importance grew it became clear that to ensure stability and growth we'd need financial support. Like all the nonprofit world, we're constantly searching for grants, sponsorships, and other kinds of support. We have a number of ideas and of course we welcome more. Fundraising is a scarce skill here — we'd appreciate your help.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>
